Our proposed prototype shows how road pressure, piezoelectric modules, electrical circuitry, storage and lighting fit together.

Proposed prototype

A road section designed around energy harvesting and storage.

This is the team's proposed prototype architecture. It shows the road surface, piezoelectric modules, electrical circuit, battery storage and LED street light in one integrated system.

PiezoPower Road prototype concept. The intended energy flow is daytime harvesting → storage → nighttime LED lighting.
Road surface

Traffic-bearing layer

Receives repeated pressure from moving vehicles and transfers controlled load to the harvesting layer.

Piezoelectric material

Energy conversion

Converts mechanical strain into electrical charge through the direct piezoelectric effect.

Storage

Battery + control

Conditions and stores daytime energy for later release to the LED load.
