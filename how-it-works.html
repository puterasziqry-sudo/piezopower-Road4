03 / SYSTEM

One complete cycle:
day to night.

The proposed system harvests energy during daylight traffic and stores it before delivering the energy to lighting after sunset.

How it works

Follow one full day-to-night energy cycle.

The system does not require the lamp to switch on every time a vehicle passes. Instead, the daytime traffic period is the harvesting period.

DAY / HARVEST

Vehicle pressure

Repeated vehicle loading activates the piezoelectric modules.

→
STORE

Battery charging

Electrical output is rectified, managed and stored.

CONTROL

Night-time release

A control stage manages delivery of stored energy.

→
NIGHT / USE

LED street light

The stored daytime energy powers the lighting load after sunset.

Technical pathway

From pressure to photons.

01

Load transfer

Road structure distributes vehicle force to the embedded module.

02

Piezoelectric effect

Mechanical strain produces electrical charge in the piezoelectric material.

03

Power conditioning

Electrical circuitry manages the generated output before storage.

04

Energy storage

Battery storage bridges daytime harvesting and nighttime lighting.
