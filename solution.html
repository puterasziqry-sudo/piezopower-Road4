02 / SOLUTION

A road system built around
harvest → store → use.

PiezoPower Road connects piezoelectric harvesting, electrical conditioning, battery storage and LED lighting into one energy cycle.

The solution

PiezoPower Road turns daytime traffic pressure into stored electrical energy.

Our solution uses protected piezoelectric materials beneath a traffic-bearing road surface. The generated electrical output is conditioned and stored in a battery, then supplied to LED street lighting after sunset.

01
INPUT

Vehicle pressure

Moving vehicles load the road surface and create repeated mechanical strain.

02
CONVERSION

Piezoelectric layer

Mechanical strain is converted into electrical charge by piezoelectric material.

03
STORAGE

Battery system

The generated electricity is conditioned and accumulated during the day.

04
OUTPUT

LED lighting

Stored energy is supplied to road lighting after sunset.

Prototype architectureRoad surface → piezoelectric material → electrical circuit → battery → LED street light
Prototype architecture

One system.
Two operating periods.

The prototype visualises the complete pathway. During the day, vehicle pressure is harvested and stored. At night, the stored energy is released to the LED street light.

Important: the prototype represents the proposed architecture. Actual road-scale energy yield must be validated experimentally for the chosen materials, traffic load and site.
